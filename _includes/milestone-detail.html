{%- comment -%} Milestone detail card - the overlay every milestone in "All Milestones Cataloged" opens into. This is a SHELL, not a template for one milestone: every field is filled in by assets/js/milestone-detail.js from the record that was clicked, because the catalog is rendered client-side from /data/milestones.json and Jekyll has never seen the record it is describing. Two deliberate choices: * It lives in its own include rather than inline in index.md so the markup and the stylesheet that targets it can be reviewed - and replaced - as a pair. * The reaction strip and the posting thread are INSIDE the card, not beside it. A reader who has not opened a milestone has no opinion about it yet, so the only thing worth showing them in the grid is the top three reactions as a passive read-out. Everything else - the full emoji set, the thread, the composer - is reachable only by opening the card. `hidden` on the root is load-bearing, not belt-and-braces: without it the panel paints during the first frame and then jumps, which reads as a flash rather than an entrance. milestone-detail.js removes it inside the opening frame. {%- endcomment -%}
