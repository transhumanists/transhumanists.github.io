{%- comment -%} ai-disclosure.html -- the AI provenance seal and the persistent "back up" control. Included once per page by _layouts/default.html, immediately before . Two elements, one style: .ai-seal a fixed white/cloudy watermark in the bottom-right corner, raised clear of the fixed AI/contact dock so it never sits on top of the footer. It is a link to the disclosure page, because a seal a visitor cannot read is decoration rather than disclosure. .ai-totop a fixed "back up" arrow on the same right-hand edge, vertically centred so it shares the horizontal plane with the seal without ever colliding with it. Hidden until assets/js/ai-seal.js has scrolled past the trigger, then persistent. Both assets are white-on-transparent SVGs from template-shared/assets/img/. Everything positional lives in assets/css/ai-seal.css; nothing here is styled inline. Parameters: href where the seal links. Defaults to site.ai_disclosure_path, then to /ai-disclosure/. A site that publishes the statement somewhere else sets `ai_disclosure_path` in _config.yml rather than editing this file, so the four copies stay byte-identical. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ai_seal_href = include.href | default: site.ai_disclosure_path | default: '/ai-disclosure/' -%}
