fix: restore the timeline's contrast, and un-break the lockfile CI reads

The timeline looked washed out. Measured rather than guessed: the responsive block
was thinning the track from the redesign's 4px to 3px and then 2px on a phone, so on a
small screen the slider was a 2px line at 0.32 alpha sitting on a 0.58 plate - there
was almost nothing left to see.

The timeline now takes its dimensions from the legend-frame rule alone, at every
breakpoint: 4px track, 14px handle, the same translucent white track with its glow,
the same plate as the legend. Six override rules are gone rather than overridden, so
there is one definition again. A test holds the floor at >=4px and >=14px and checks
the plate still shares the legend's blur and radius, because that shared frame is the
whole point of the redesign.

One judgement call worth flagging: the redesign also lifted the timeline 60px off the
bottom edge on a phone. I kept it docked at the bottom instead, because the legend now
lives in that band - restoring the lift leaves a 141px-tall phone map with about 25px
between the two controls. Say the word and I will restore the lift and re-place the
legend above it.

Also fixed, and this one had main red: running `bun install` under Bun 1.4.0 rewrote
bun.lock in the newer text format, which CI's pinned Bun 1.2.14 cannot parse, so every
job in the browser-layout workflow died at "Install deps" without running a check. The
lockfile is regenerated in the version the pinned toolchain reads, and three tests now
assert it stays that way: that bun.lock is JSON rather than the text format, that its
version matches the oldest Bun CI pins, and that the job which installs dependencies
pins Bun explicitly. package.json is asserted too, since the lockfile referenced
@playwright/test while no manifest existed for it to belong to.

19 workflow tests, 180 unit tests, 30 browser checks green on Bun 1.2.14 and 1.4.0.
